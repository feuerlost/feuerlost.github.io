I am an undergraduate student majoring in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS). My research interests lie at the intersection of **brain-computer interfaces (BCI)** and **artificial intelligence (AI)**. In particular, I am interested in learning behavior-relevant representations from neural activity and using them to enable intelligent embodied control and neuroprosthetic systems.

My current research focuses on neural representation learning for brain-embodied manipulation, including neural decoding from M1/PMd spiking activity and the integration of neural intent with vision-language-action models. I have also worked on computer vision pipelines for visual prostheses, involving depth estimation, semantic segmentation, and phosphene simulation.

I plan to apply to Ph.D. programs for **Fall 2028**. I am always happy to connect with faculty, researchers, and students working on BCI, neural decoding, embodied AI, or neuroprosthetics. If you are interested in discussing potential research opportunities or collaborations, please feel free to [contact me](mailto:zhangzeyu24@mails.ucas.ac.cn).
